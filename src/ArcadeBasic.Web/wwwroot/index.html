Arcade BASIC

      
A Full BASIC (ANSI X3.113 / ECMA-116) interpreter and bytecode VM written in C#; here it runs
        in your browser, compiled to WebAssembly. Your programs stay on your computer: they're kept in
        this browser and in the files you save. Nothing is sent anywhere.

      
The editor is CodeMirror.

Click the piano, or play it on the keyboard (A W S E D F T G Y H U J K), to add notes where the
        cursor is in the string; Space plays it. Notes are the string's Music Macro Language: A–G with # or -, a
        length (4 a quarter, 8 an eighth …), O octave, < > down or up an octave, P a rest, T tempo, L default length.
